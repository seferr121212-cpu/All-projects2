# FOG BELL — Steam store copy (EN)

**Short description (300 char limit):**
1977, Swallow Island. Your brother Yusuf kept the lighthouse, and now he's gone. You have an oil lamp, Echoes in the fog that freeze in the light, and a bell that has stayed silent for forty years for a reason.

**About:**
Kerem returns to the island after twelve years. The village is empty, the lighthouse is dark, and all his brother left behind are letters.

Fog Bell is an atmospheric, story-driven 2D puzzle-platformer where light is both your weapon and your lifeline.

- **The rule of light:** Echoes freeze in your lamp's glow. Run out of oil and the dark finds you.
- **Four kinds of Echo:** Crawlers, Mimics (they call out in voices you love), Watchers (take the light off them and they strike), Whisperers (your lamp can't stop them).
- **Light puzzles:** mirrors, lenses, red glass, a hand bell, braziers and the lighthouse mechanism itself.
- **Stealth:** crouch, hold your breath, throw pebbles to lure Echoes away.
- **Hand-drawn pixel art** with real-time light and shadow, rain, fog and storms.
- **An island's memory:** 12 journal pages, 6 villagers' letters, tapes, radio broadcasts and a playable chapter from the winter of 1953.
- **Two endings and a hidden epilogue.**
- Full English and Turkish text, subtitle sizes, sound captions, key rebinding, three difficulty levels and gamepad support.
